Delete your UltraZeta account

You can also do this in the game: Levels → Community → Account → Delete account.

1. Your email address

Enter the address you signed in with. We'll email you a 6-digit code to prove it's yours.

2. Confirm the deletion

We sent a code to . It is valid for 10 minutes. Check your spam folder if it doesn't arrive.

This is deleted immediately and can't be undone:

  • your account and display name
  • the link between your email and the account
  • all sign-ins on every device

Levels and packs you published stay, shown as made by "a deleted player" with nothing linking them to you. To remove them too, take them down in the game first (Account → Take down).

Your account is deleted.

If the game is still installed, it will ask you to sign in again the next time you open the Community tab. Signing in with the same address later creates a new, empty account.
