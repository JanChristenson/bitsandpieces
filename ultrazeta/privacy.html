UltraZeta Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

In short

  • You can play the whole game without an account. Nothing about you leaves your device.
  • An account is only needed to publish levels. We never store your email address, only a hash of it.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics, nothing sold or shared.
  • You can delete your account at any time, in the game or on the web.

Who we are

UltraZeta is made by Jan Christensson ("we"), Sweden. Questions about this policy or your data: bitsandpieces.indie@gmail.com.

Playing without an account

You can play every level, use the level editor and download community levels and packs without an account. Your progress, settings and own levels are stored only on your device. Community levels you download are fetched from our server; those requests contain no personal data beyond what any internet connection reveals (see Reports and safety).

Community account (optional)

To publish levels and packs you create an account. We process:

  • Email address – used only to send you a 6-digit sign-in code. To deliver it we pass the address to our email provider, Mailjet. We do not store your email address. We keep only a one-way cryptographic hash of it, so we can recognise your account the next time you sign in.
  • Display name – chosen by you and shown publicly with your levels and packs.
  • Account id and sign-in tokens – to keep you signed in. Sign-in tokens are stored as hashes and expire after 90 days without use.

The legal basis is the performance of the service you ask for (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).

Content you publish

Levels and packs you publish are public: anyone can play them, together with your display name. The solution you submit when publishing is used only to check that the level can be solved; it is never shown to other players.

Reports and safety

When you report a level or pack we store the report, the reason and any note you write, together with your account id or, if you are not signed in, a one-way hash of your IP address. Hashed IP addresses are also used for short-lived counters that limit abuse, such as too many sign-in attempts. Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in keeping the community safe (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).

What we don't do

  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs, and no device identifiers.
  • No location, contacts, photos or other device data.
  • We never sell or share your data with anyone for their own purposes.

Where your data is stored

On Microsoft Azure servers in the EU (North Europe region). Sign-in emails are sent by Mailjet, an EU-based email service. Both act only on our behalf. All connections between the game and our server are encrypted (HTTPS).

How long we keep it

  • Account data: until you delete your account.
  • Sign-in codes: 10 minutes.
  • Abuse-limit counters: they reset after an hour (a day for reports).
  • Technical server logs: up to 30 days, then deleted automatically.

Deleting your account

How

  • In the game: Levels → Community → Account → Delete account, then confirm.
  • Without the game: enter your email address on the deletion page and type the code we send you.
Delete my account on the web

Deleted immediately and permanently

  • your account and account id;
  • your display name (it becomes free for others to use);
  • the hashed email address that linked your email to the account;
  • all sign-in sessions and any pending sign-in code.

What stays

  • Levels and packs you published remain playable, shown as made by "a deleted player". Nothing links them to you or your email any more. To remove them too, take them down in the game (Account → Take down) before deleting your account, or email us.
  • Reports you sent stay so moderation keeps working, but the account id on them no longer points to anyone.
  • Technical server logs expire as described above.

Signing in again with the same email address afterwards creates a new, empty account.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask for access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, object to processing based on legitimate interest, and receive your data in a portable format. Email bitsandpieces.indie@gmail.com. You can also complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY).

Children

UltraZeta is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and the date at the top. Significant changes will also be announced in the game.
